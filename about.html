About

Hi! I'm Dottie.

My background in human-computer interaction design helps me combine systems-level thinking, creativity, and data analysis to solve complex challenges. My work focuses on the relationship between information, technology, and people and how we can bridge the gaps in between. With a strong foundation in human-computer interaction design, I am currently helping build and maintain a scalable, enterprise-level design system. Examples of recent work includes designing user flows and interfaces for context and reasoning across the product suite, documenting UI patterns, and facilitating team workshops. Skilled in cross-team collaboration, research, and project management.

I hold a B.S. and M.S. from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill in Information Science and a B.A. in French. Continuing to further my education as a 2nd year PhD student studying the sociotechnical implications of AI.
